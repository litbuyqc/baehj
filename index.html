The Oopbuy spreadsheet guide

Research first.
Decide second.

A useful shortlist keeps comparable information in one place. These steps work whether you use a spreadsheet, a notes app or a saved product list.

Find a starting point on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose one category and a clear brief

    Set the item type, preferred color, fit and budget. Compare similar items before expanding your search.

  2. Capture the exact listing and variant

    Save the source URL, seller name, selected color, size, displayed price and the date you checked it. Avoid mixing information from different variants.

  3. Compare measurements and inspection photos

    Use actual measurements where available. If your service provides quality-control photos, inspect the selected variant, visible finish and any requested measurements.

  4. Estimate the complete delivered cost

    Include applicable delivery charges, service fees, shipping, taxes and currency conversion. Confirm the service's current terms and destination restrictions before paying.

Answers before checkout

A few useful
clarifications.

Understand what a product list can tell you, and what still needs checking.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize links, categories, prices and comparison notes for an Oopbuy-related shopping workflow. The term does not by itself mean that a list is official, current or independently verified.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and research guide. Its category links open CAFANS listings. You can use the comparison fields described here to build your own spreadsheet.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing's actual measurements with a garment or pair you already own. For clothing, record chest, length, shoulder and waist measurements where relevant. For shoes, compare the seller's sizing guidance and foot-length information rather than relying only on a regional size label.

Do brand names or photographs prove authenticity?

No. A brand name, product photograph or inspection image alone does not establish authenticity. Check seller identity, authorization where applicable, product documentation and return terms before buying.

Is the listed item price the final delivered cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the service and destination, the total may include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes, duties and currency conversion costs. Confirm current charges before paying.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent research and category guide linking to CAFANS. It is not presented as an official Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website.
